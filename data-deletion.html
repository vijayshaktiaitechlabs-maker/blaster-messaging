How to delete your data

Last updated: 24 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the data Blaster Messaging holds about you or your business at any time. Use whichever way below suits you.

If you are a Blaster Messaging customer

  1. Disconnect WhatsApp: in Blaster Messaging, open Settings, WhatsApp number, and choose Disconnect. We delete the access token for your WhatsApp Business Account straight away and stop all sending.
  2. Delete your workspace: in Settings, choose Delete workspace. We delete your contacts, templates, campaigns, messages and reports within 7 days, and from backups within 30 days. Invoices are kept only as long as Indian tax law requires.
  3. Or email us: write to contactus@orena.solutions with the subject "Data deletion request" from the email address on your account. We confirm deletion within 30 days.

If you connected through Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook account, Settings and privacy, Settings, Business Integrations (or Apps and websites).
  2. Find Blaster Messaging and choose Remove.
  3. Meta tells us about the removal and we delete the data we received from Meta for your account within 30 days. You can also email us to confirm.

If you received messages from a business that uses Blaster Messaging

  • Reply STOP to that business on WhatsApp to stop receiving its campaigns.
  • To have your number and messages deleted, ask that business, or email contactus@orena.solutions with your phone number and the business name. We pass the request to the business and help it delete your data.
